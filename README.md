# Janhavi — 3D Interactive World (Adventurer Journey) 🚶☀️

A minimal, stylized 3D interactive portfolio world inspired by **Bruno Simon's** iconic clay aesthetic, featuring a reference-matched **"Simplified Neutral Adventurer - Girl"** low-poly character walking along the path through Janhavi's milestones.

---

## 🎨 Features & Reference Match

- **Reference Model**: Low-poly character modeled directly from the **"Simplified Neutral Adventurer - Girl"** sheet:
  - **Tunic & Inner Shirt**: Flared cream sleeveless over-tunic (`#FFFDD0`) with inner dark brown shirt (`#8B4513`).
  - **Shorts & Backpack**: Olive moss green shorts & hiking backpack (`#6B8E23`) with rolled bedroll sleeping mat (`#E0D7C6`) and buckle straps.
  - **Head & Ponytail**: Sandy ash-brown hair (`#C5A880`) swept back into a low-poly topknot/ponytail, faceted neutral face, and eyebrows.
  - **Hiking Gear**: Hand-carved wooden walking stick in hand, and sturdy dark brown hiking boots (`#4A3525`).
  - **Procedural Gait**: Smooth walking cycle with leg stride swings, arm swings, body bobbing, and ponytail bounce.
- **Bruno Simon Clay Aesthetic**: Warm terracotta/peach floor, soft directional sun shadows, faceted chartreuse/yellow trees, and chalky white boulders.
- **Big 3D Sculpture**: A chunky extruded 3D white text sculpture reading **"JANHAVI"** at the starting area.
- **Road & Milestone Buildings**:
  1. **Schooling**: PSBA English School, Aurangabad (schoolhouse with bell tower).
  2. **Engineering**: MITAOE Campus (collegiate hall with portico pillars).
  3. **1st Year**: Maker Lab & Prototyping (modern workshop with solar roof).
  4. **Second Year**: Coppercloud HQ (contemporary glass office block).
  5. **Third Year**: Coppercloud R&D (perception inspection lab with radar dome).
  6. **Fourth Year**: Clarity Lab, IIT Jodhpur (desert pavilion with 360° panorama dome).
  7. **Current Research**: Spatial Digital Twins Lab (futuristic pavilion with floating 3D voxel prism).
- **Small Click Popups**: Clicking on any building displays a clean, minimal floating popup card directly anchored over the building with key details.
- **Controls**:
  - `W` / `Up Arrow`: Walk forward
  - `S` / `Down Arrow`: Walk backward
  - `A` / `Left Arrow`: Turn left
  - `D` / `Right Arrow`: Turn right
  - Click on any building to view details; click ground to dismiss popup.
  - Reset button to jump back to spawn.
