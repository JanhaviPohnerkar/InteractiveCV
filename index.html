    🚀 The Engineering Story

    I'm an Electronics & Telecommunication engineering student who loves taking ideas all the way from raw analog sensor signals to robust deployed systems. My work spans computer vision, visual SLAM, embedded firmware, and edge AI.

    I've built and deployed an industrial defect-detection system for a US-based manufacturer, run ORB-SLAM3 on a Raspberry Pi 4B and Jetson Orin Nano with ARM-level optimizations, built a working EMG-controlled bionic prosthetic arm, and conducted research-style work in shape-from-shading and medical imaging.
